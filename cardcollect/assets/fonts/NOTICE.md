Fonts in this directory (DejaVuSans.ttf, DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf) are from the
DejaVu Fonts project (https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/), copied here from a
system install of the `fonts-dejavu-core` package rather than downloaded
directly (this build environment has no outbound access to arbitrary
websites, only a fixed package-registry allowlist).

DejaVu fonts are free/libre and explicitly permit redistribution and bundling
with software, including modification, under their own license (a derivative
of the Bitstream Vera license). The full license text was not available to
copy verbatim from this sandbox -- before shipping this cog publicly, fetch
the authoritative license text from https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
and place it here as LICENSE, per the project's own redistribution terms.
